01 / THE RECORD

BEFORE THE
HISTORY,
THE IDENTITY.

STILL BREATHING is where WEST THEORY begins. Before the catalogue turns toward the history of hip-hop, the first record establishes the project on its own terms.

West Coast soul sits at the foundation. Melody, weight, attitude and low-end pressure meet a sound that already refuses to stay completely inside one box.

This is the introduction rather than the history lesson. Eight tracks establish the identity before BUILT FROM BREAKS turns backward to the breakbeats, DJs, MCs and foundations that made everything after them possible.
